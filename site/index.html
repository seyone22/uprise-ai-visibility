Appendix

Method and definitions

How the 280 answers were collected

  • 7 AI Search buying questions (GEO, AI SEO, ChatGPT/Perplexity Visibility, Google AI Overviews, AEO/Entity SEO, B2B Legal/Construction/Solar AI Search, AI Visibility Audits) × 8 Australian markets = 56 market-intent pairs.
  • Each pair was evaluated across 5 AI answer engines (Google AI Mode, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity Sonar, Gemini 3.8, and Claude Sonnet 4.6) for N = 280 total live answers.
  • 56 matching Australian Google SERP queries were executed via Firecrawl (country: 'au') to record exact Top-10 organic rankings and citation overlap.
  • All live URLs on uprisedigital.com.au were crawled to inspect schema triples, internal links, and copy consistency.

How answers and citations were scored

  • Every agency named in an answer was extracted in order of first appearance and resolved to its canonical entity name.
  • Grounding URLs and Vertex AI redirect titles were resolved to root domains in pipeline/citations.json.
  • A “leaked citation” occurs when an engine cites uprisedigital.com.au in its grounding sources (61 answers) but omits Uprise Digital from the recommended agencies in the response text (25 answers).
  • Share of Model Voice (SoMV) is the percentage of answers naming an agency out of 280 nationally (or out of 35 per market), bounded with 95% Wilson confidence intervals.
